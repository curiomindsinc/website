🧩 How to build
  1. Drag ions from the tray onto the board. Tapping a tray ion also adds it.
  2. Keep adding cations (+) and anions (−) until the pieces fit with no gaps: overall charge 0.
  3. To remove an ion, drag it off the board or tap it.
  4. Press Check.

Example

Crossover method

  1. Write the two ions with their charges.
  2. Cross over the charge numbers. Ignore the signs.
  3. Simplify to the lowest whole-number ratio.
  4. Write the formula. Brackets go round a polyatomic ion used more than once. Leave out any 1.
  5. Check: total positive charge = total negative charge.
🎮 How to play
  1. A compound name appears. Tap ion tiles to build its formula.
  2. Tap a tile again to add another of that ion, e.g. Cl− ×2. Tap − on a tile to remove one.
  3. No hints while you build: you only see what went wrong after a wrong answer.
  4. Press Check. You have 35 s per compound and 4 min in total.

    ⚠️ Common mistakes
    • Writing MgCl: the charges must balance, so Mg2+ needs two Cl− → MgCl2.
    • Writing Mg2O2: always simplify the ratio → MgO.
    • Writing CaOH2: that means one OH and two extra H. Use brackets → Ca(OH)2.
    • Writing a charge in the formula, e.g. Na+Cl−: a compound is neutral, so formulae show no charges.
    • Mixing up iron(II) Fe2+ and iron(III) Fe3+: the Roman numeral is the charge.
