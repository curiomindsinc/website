Equation Balancer

Balancing equations · O-Level Chemistry

Equation

🧩 How to balance
  1. Press + and − to change the big numbers in front (coefficients).
  2. Never change the small numbers (subscripts): that would make a different substance.
  3. Use the Atom count table to compare each element on both sides.
  4. When every element matches, press Check. The next equation comes up by itself.
💡 Stuck?

Hint gives a clue. Press it again for a bigger clue. Answer shows the balanced equation.

Example

The method

  1. Count the atoms of each element on both sides.
  2. Put 1 in front of the most complicated substance.
  3. Balance one element at a time. Leave H and O, and lone elements like O2, until last.
  4. A fraction is fine for now. Multiply everything at the end to get whole numbers.
  5. Check every element, and that the numbers are the lowest whole-number ratio.
🎮 How to play
  1. Balance as many equations as you can in 3 minutes.
  2. No atom count while you play: count the atoms yourself.
  3. Press Check. A wrong answer shows what is off, and you can try again for half points.
  4. Skip moves on but breaks your streak.
