Electricity Lab

Four linked simulations covering the whole electricity strand of upper-secondary Physics. Students build circuits, watch charge move, predict meter readings and test them, then work through chapter challenges and exam-style questions. Each sim has a free-build sandbox.

Written for Singapore-Cambridge O-Level Physics (6091), with Cambridge IGCSE references. Symbol view uses exam-style circuit symbols; Real view shows illustrated parts. Every file works offline, on phones and tablets, and inside SLS.

Three misconceptions, tested head-on

  • “Adding a parallel branch adds resistance.” Students predict the ammeter reading before a branch is added (D.C. Circuits, chapter c).
  • “A battery supplies a constant current.” The same battery drives different loads; the e.m.f. stays the same while the current changes (Current, P.D. & Resistance, chapter c).
  • “The fuse and the earth wire do the same job.” Fault scenarios separate their roles (Practical Electricity, chapter e).
